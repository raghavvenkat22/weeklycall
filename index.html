What the account is telling us

  • Conversion is holding; the pressure is auction cost. CPM rose 54% from January to October while CVR held in a 2.5% to 3.2% band and AOV rose to $125.94. Action: answer cost with creative volume and broader prospecting.

  • The ShopOS overlay catalog is the best prospecting format. Overlays read 1.30 and 1.21 on 30-day GA4 vs 0.94 for the plain catalog, and the overlay Lookalike improved 24% week on week. Action: move the Lookalike into Catalog+Conv with the overlay, scale the overlay LAL 25% and launch three product-set overlay ad sets.
